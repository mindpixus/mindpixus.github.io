Mission brief

Three things nobody else in Grade 5 science is doing at once.

The animation

Pixar-caliber, not clip-art.

Every scene is built to feel like a real animated film — not a slideshow with a narrator, not a cardboard mascot. If it wouldn't hold up next to a real Pixar short, it doesn't ship.

The story

One continuing show, not disconnected clips.

Nova and Dua's timeline keeps unfolding lesson to lesson. Kids come back to find out what happens next — the science just happens to be true.

The curriculum

Built on the actual standard.

Sequenced to NGSS, topic by topic, the way a homeschool parent or teacher actually plans a year — not a random grab-bag of "cool facts" videos.

The story so far

A closed loop, seen from both ends.

In the present, a robot named Nova shows up to guide a curious ten-year-old named Dua through the science she's determined to question. In the future, Dr. Dua — grown, and a scientist — builds Nova and sends her back. Neither of them knows the whole story yet. That's the point.

NOVA · guide

Dry humor, warm underneath it, and gaps in her memory from the trip back that she's not always in a hurry to explain.

DUA · scientist-to-be

Quick to guess, often wrong first, stubborn about finding out why — and keeps a field notebook on everything Nova won't say.
